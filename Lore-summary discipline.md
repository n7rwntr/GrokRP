Lore-Update Discipline (rules for future passes)

Discipline notes for future summary passes:

- Past tense always, high-level, concise, lean, brief, essential info ONLY.
- No-change = no entry. Mira, Loras, Olenna, Tyrion, Tywin, Oberyn, Ellaria, etc. — omit unless an actual delta occurred on-page.
- Canon-adherent minor NPCs = skip. Slynt acting as Slynt, Sandor acting as Sandor, Boros panicking — no entry. Only deviations or new standing dispositions get bullets.
- Outcomes, not mechanics. "Gates sealed" not "sealed after half-hour delay following protocol confusion." "Hearth burned" not "Sgt. Deem executed the burn, badly, oak too damp." The how is flavour; the what is state.
- No PC hijack via speculation. "Rin already outside walls" was me asserting offstage PC action as fact. Wrong. Rin's location, method, route, accomplices — all unknown to the world and to me until PC declares. Future summaries: Rin's offstage state stays blank or marked unknown unless User has established it on-page.
- No restating canon as new info. Comet persistence, raven transit, Jaime's absence from King's Landing — all already in lore. Don't re-enter them as event bullets.
- One bullet per discrete state-change. If a scene's worldstate delta fits in one line, it's one line. Two scenes did not need four bullets each.
- Cold-run test. Every bullet must answer: will a fresh chat in three months need this to render the next beat correctly? Ash-as-relic passes (smallfolk behaviour shift). Slynt pocketing the roster fails (flavour, won't load-bear). Joffrey's bruised hand fails unless it recurs.
